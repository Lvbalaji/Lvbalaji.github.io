Who Am I?

I am a results-driven cybersecurity professional with a specialized focus on Offensive Security, Active Directory exploitation, and Network Penetration Testing. My approach is rooted in the adversarial mindset—understanding exactly how attackers operate to build stronger defenses.

Currently based in Ireland, I am pursuing my MSc in Cybersecurity at Munster Technological University. When I am not in the classroom, I am deep in a terminal, sharpening my skills on PortSwigger (170+ labs) and HackTheBox.

Certifications

  • OSCP+: Full-scale testing, rapid vulnerability ID, and reporting (Jan 2025).
  • CPTS: 10-day pentest against simulated enterprise networks & AD infrastructure.
  • CRTP: AD exploitation, enumeration, and privilege escalation.
  • OSWP: WEP, WPA/WPA2, and Enterprise network attacks.

Research & Projects

Unleashing Fileless Malware on Windows: ML-Powered Defence

I designed a Machine Learning pipeline to analyze fileless malware techniques.
